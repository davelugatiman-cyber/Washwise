WashWise — Laundry Service Tracker

Tagline: "Your Laundry, Our Care. Track It with Ease."

Community Problem

Laundry customers may have difficulty checking service prices before visiting a shop and may need to contact the shop repeatedly to ask whether their clothes are ready. This can waste time for both customers and staff.

Gap in Current Solutions

Some laundry shops may not provide a single, convenient online place for checking prices, estimating costs, requesting pickup, and searching for order status.

Proposed Solution

WashWise provides a simple website where customers can view laundry services and sample prices, calculate an estimated bill, submit a pickup request, and search for a sample order status.

Target Users

Customers who use laundry services and want to check prices, arrange pickup, and check whether their laundry is ready.

Expected Benefits

  • Customers save time by checking prices and status online.
  • Small laundry businesses can organize pickup requests and promote services.
  • Clear sample pricing helps customers budget before visiting.
